# No em dashes

Never use em dashes (`—`) in authored text. This covers all prose written for
the project: page and component content, documentation, READMEs, changelog
entries, and commit and PR descriptions.

Use a comma, a colon, parentheses, or a separate sentence instead, whichever
fits the sentence. Reserve the regular hyphen (`-`) for compound words and
ranges only; do not substitute a spaced hyphen as a stand-in for an em dash.
